# Development Roadmap

Planned feature enhancements, module upgrades, and long-term milestones for Orchid Tracker.

---

## Near-Term Goals (v1.5)
* **Photo Attachment Storage**: Ability to attach high-resolution images to individual specimen passport cards with local asset optimization.
* **CSV/JSON Data Export**: One-click exports of active inventory ledgers and bloom history logs for external data analysis.
* **Enhanced Repotting Reminders**: Automated visual dashboard alerts for plants nearing or past their calculated repotting due dates.

---

## Long-Term Vision (v2.0)
* **Greenhouse Environment Dashboard**: Integration options for microclimate environmental sensors (tracking humidity, temperature, and light levels in foot-candles).
* **Care Forecasting Engine**: Predictive care calendars analyzing seasonal bloom trends and watering frequency patterns per genus.
* **Advanced Archival Analytics**: Long-term survival, cost analysis, and vendor reliability metrics based on historical archive data.
