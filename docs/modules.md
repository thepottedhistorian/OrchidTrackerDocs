# Core Modules & Components

Overview of the primary functional modules within Orchid Tracker.

* **Specimen Management:** Log individual orchid records, accession numbers, genera, species/hybrids, and lineage details.
* **Environmental & Telemetry Tracker:** Monitor microclimate parameters including temperature, humidity, light exposure, and ventilation schedules.
* **Care & Maintenance Logging:** Track watering, fertilization routines, potting media composition, and repotting history.
* **Health & Pest Control:** Record treatments, disease observations, and quarantine status.
