# Core Modules & Components

Overview of the primary operational modules contained within the Orchid Tracker interface.

## Modules Summary
* **Specimen Management:** Log individual plant records, accession numbers, genera, species/hybrids, and lineage details.
* **Environmental & Telemetry Tracker:** Monitor enclosure microclimate parameters including temperature, humidity, light exposure, and ventilation schedules.
* **Care & Maintenance Logging:** Track watering, fertilization routines (e.g., custom well-water formulations), potting media composition, and container configurations.
* **Health & Pest Control:** Record treatments, health observations, and plant quarantine status.
